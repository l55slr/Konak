- **Code** (the application under `/app`, `/components`, `/lib`, `/scripts`,

## Why CC0?

Directory data and migration guides are most useful when they can travel:
mirrored, translated, forked, printed, and folded into other projects
without friction. A privacy resource that restricts copying would be working
against itself.
